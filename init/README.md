Sampling of initial conditions from a Wigner distribution and setting up the initial energy calculations

Initial coordinates and velocities for the trajectories are generated by sampling randomly from the Wigner distribution of the ground state nuclear wave function (in the harmonic approximation), which can be calculated from the vibrational frequencies and normal modes

We want to determine the initial excited state from where the dynamics starts To find the initial states after vertical excitation, it is necessary to obtain the excitation energies and oscillator strengths for all initial geometries
